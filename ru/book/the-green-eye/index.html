Прочитать на английском

The halls of the Tower must never fall silent. Nobody tells the apprentices why.

Sixteen-year-old Darin Sivel, a docker's son, is a dead hand: a convict servant of the Conclave, sentenced for life to sweep up after magic he may never touch. He is also the only person alive who can stop a spell with a single word, and each time he does, a little more feeling dies in his right hand.

Now the docks are at war with the Tower. Iskra, the smuggler's daughter Darin owes a debt, leads a movement called the Hook. Her boatmen and dockers have shut the Tower's quay and left its crates to rot in the roads. Through the dead hands inside, she is putting the halls out one by one.

Silent halls starve whatever sleeps beneath the Tower, and a starving thing pushes. Stone climbs up through the thresholds of the Fish Market, and people are going still in their own doorways.

The First Magister sends Darin to break the blockade. In the Tower, Talior Escrin, the apprentice who caught him in the warehouse, ties a white band round his sleeve, and the sons of the old houses follow. Iskra is right that the Conclave pays for its secrets with the docks. She is wrong about what silence will cost.

Darin has always told himself he belongs to no one. Both sides are about to prove him wrong.

How much of a city can one dead hand hold?
